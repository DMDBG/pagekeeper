Pagekeeper

An ad-free reader for your EPUB, PDF and text books, with audiobooks that follow along. Your place syncs between iPhone and PC.

With a signing service (Signulous and similar)

  1. Tap Download the IPA in Safari. It saves to the Files app, in Downloads.
  2. In your signing app, choose to upload or import an app, and pick Pagekeeper.ipa from Files. If it can install from a link, paste the IPA link instead (use Copy the IPA link).
  3. Sign and install. When Pagekeeper says a new version is out, do the same again; your books and progress stay.

With AltStore or SideStore updates itself

Add to AltStore Add to SideStore

Other signers that take AltStore-style sources (repos), like Feather, ESign or Scarlet, can add the same source link. Updates then show up in that app.

From a PC with Sideloadly

Download the IPA on your PC, plug in your iPhone, and drag the IPA into Sideloadly. With a free Apple ID, re-sign it every 7 days.
